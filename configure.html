Download macro_hub.uf2
  1. Double-tap the reset button on the hub — the LED will pulse to show it's in bootloader mode.
  2. A USB drive named NICENANO appears on your computer.
  3. Drag the downloaded macro_hub.uf2 onto that drive.
  4. The drive disappears and the hub restarts automatically — you're done.
