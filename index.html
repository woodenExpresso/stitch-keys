How
they attach

Each key has 4 magnetic pogo connectors. These magnets allow you to attach & detach these keys easily. Once they snap together, they are able to get and send information to the hub.

Macro Recorder

Record any sequence of key presses and mouse clicks, edit the timing, then play it back — from any app — with one keystroke. Free desktop app for Windows & Mac.

REC
Record & play Capture keyboard and mouse events globally, then replay them exactly.
ED
Edit timing Double-click any event to adjust its delay. Reorder, duplicate, or delete rows.
2x
Repeat & speed Loop a macro N times at 0.25× to 4× speed. Add a countdown before it starts.
F9
Global hotkey Press F9 from any application to trigger playback without switching windows.
Z
Undo & insert 50-step undo history. Insert key presses or mouse clicks at any position.
XY
Relative mouse Offset all mouse coordinates so your macro works wherever your window is.
